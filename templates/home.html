Lifecycle State Machine

How Campus Complaints Work

Every ticket moves through an auditable 4-stage transition sequence enforced strictly on the server:

Step 1
Student Reports
Status: SUBMITTED

Students submit complaints specifying category, location, priority, and optional photos of the issue.

Step 2
Admin Assigns
Status: ASSIGNED

Campus Administrators review incoming complaints and assign the ticket to the qualified maintenance staff member.

Step 3
Staff Resolves
IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED

Assigned staff starts work and submits mandatory resolution remarks upon completing the maintenance task.

Step 4
Student Closes
Status: CLOSED

The reporting student (or administrator) verifies the physical fix and permanently closes the ticket.
